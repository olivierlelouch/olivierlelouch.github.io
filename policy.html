  • 2026 [Cover]

    Hooked on Subsidies: The Case for Reform

    World Bank, Washington, DC, 2026. Contributor.

    Shows that emerging market and developing economies spend heavily on subsidies, often more than on health and education combined, while most subsidies miss their intended objectives. It argues that full accounting of subsidies, systematic cost-benefit assessment and attention to the political economy of reform can free up fiscal space and improve outcomes through better targeting and design.

  • 2025

    The Scoping Workshop Report on the International Governance Structures for Low-Carbon Investment Risk Guarantees in EMDEs

    Y. Melekh and O. Lelouch. Workshop report, UCL Institute for Sustainable Resources, UCL Centre for Law and Environment and Chatham House, September 2025.

    Draws on a scoping workshop to examine how risk guarantees could scale up low-carbon investment in emerging and developing economies. It finds that their effectiveness depends less on financial engineering than on governance: regulatory fragmentation, supervisory practice and geopolitical constraints. It closes with open research questions, including how guarantees could become a liquid asset class that mobilises private capital rather than only protecting investors.

  • 2022

    Scaling Clean Energy Through Climate Finance Innovation: Structure of an Exchange Rate Coverage Facility for Developing Countries

    P. Benoit, J. Elkind, L. Beltran, J. Roche, E. Mehlum, O. Lelouch and C. S. Sinha. Center on Global Energy Policy, Columbia University, with the World Bank and the World Economic Forum, October 2022.

    Proposes an Exchange Rate Coverage Facility that would guarantee international lenders against losses from local-currency depreciation, a major barrier to foreign finance for clean energy in developing countries. The facility would be funded by monetised carbon credits, multilateral development banks and philanthropic and private capital, lowering the cost of capital without adding to host governments’ fiscal burden.

  • 2021 [Cover]

    Global Hydrogen Review 2021

    International Energy Agency, Paris, 2021. Contributor.

    The IEA’s first annual review of hydrogen production, demand, policy and investment worldwide. It tracks progress against net-zero pathways and identifies the areas where policy action is most needed to accelerate deployment.

  • 2021

    Oil Market Report: April 2021

    International Energy Agency, Paris, April 2021. Contributor.

    The IEA’s monthly assessment of global oil supply, demand, stocks and prices. This edition forecast a strong rebound in oil demand in 2021 as economies recovered from Covid-19, alongside the OPEC+ decision to raise output gradually.

  • 2020

    Liaison ferroviaire européenne Lyon-Turin : pistes de financement [Lyon-Turin European Rail Link: Avenues for Funding]

    A. Aymé, L. Clavurier, C. Maggi and O. Lelouch. Comité pour la Transalpine and Sia Partners, July 2020. In French.

    Makes the case for the Lyon-Turin freight and passenger rail link as a priority low-carbon infrastructure project for the post-Covid recovery, in line with the European Green Deal. It reviews the agreed and potential sources of funding: EU funds, contributions from the French state and local authorities, the Eurovignette directive and similar mechanisms, and borrowing from the European Investment Bank through loans or project bonds. From these it builds three financing scenarios, each limiting the call on the French state budget to around €200 million a year, or €4–6 billion in total.

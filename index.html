About Me

I am an Economist and PhD Candidate at UCL, specializing in the intersection of sovereign debt sustainability and the macroeconomics of the energy transition.

My research examines how macroeconomic policy can support fiscal resilience and economic stability during the global shift toward low-carbon economies.

Previously, I led and contributed to policy and analytical work at the World Bank as a Macroeconomist in the Fiscal Unit, focusing on fiscal aspects of low-carbon policies. I also worked at the International Energy Agency (IEA) as an Analyst in the Oil Industry Market Division.

I hold a Master’s degree from the University of Paris-Saclay in Energy Economics and a ‘Grande École’ Engineering Master’s degree from IMT Atlantique (formerly École des Mines de Nantes & Télécom Bretagne) in France.
